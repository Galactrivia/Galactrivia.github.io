Galactrivia Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: Galactrivia has no accounts and no server of its own. Your scores and settings stay on your own phone. Only if you turn on Pro mode of the sky map yourself does the app use your approximate location, and that also stays on your phone. To keep the app free, Galactrivia shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes data about your device to do this. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings in the app.

1. Who is responsible?

Galactrivia is made by Wouter Bisseling, the Netherlands.
Privacy contact: galactrivia@gmail.com

2. Data that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your own device:

  • your daily puzzle scores and results, and your streak (days played in a row);
  • your settings (light/dark, language);
  • whether you bought Galactrivia Premium.

This data is not sent to us and we cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in Android settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Galactrivia uses Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to show ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google:

DataExample
IP addressmay indicate your general location (country/region)
Device and account identifiersthe Android advertising ID and app set ID
App interactionsfor example app launches and taps on ads
Diagnosticsfor example app and SDK performance

Purpose: showing and measuring ads, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS).

Consent: in the EEA and UK you will see a consent message from Google when you first open the app. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent. Without consent you get limited or non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Location and motion sensors (Pro mode of the sky map)

The sky map has a Pro mode that shows which stars, planets, the Sun and the Moon are above your location right now. Only when you turn this mode on yourself, the app first explains why, and then Android asks for permission to use your approximate location (accurate to a few kilometres). The app never asks for your precise location or for location in the background.

  • Purpose: only to point the star map correctly (which sky is above you right now).
  • Where: the calculation happens only on your phone. Your location is not stored, not sent to us or anyone else, and not used for anything else.
  • Motion sensors: the app reads your phone's compass and motion sensor so the map turns with you. This also stays on your phone. Android does not ask permission for this.
  • Withdrawing: you can withdraw the permission at any time in Android settings (Apps → Galactrivia → Permissions → Location). The normal sky map and the games work without location.

Your approximate location is determined by your phone's location service (on most Android phones, Google's services). How Google handles this is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases

Galactrivia Premium is purchased through Google Play. We never receive payment details such as your name, address or card. Google Play only tells the app whether the purchase was made, so ads can be removed and the extra features unlocked. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalized ads.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for limited or non-personalized ads and fraud prevention, so the app can be offered for free.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for delivering a purchased upgrade.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for using your approximate location in Pro mode; you give and withdraw this consent through Android.

7. How long is data kept?

Data on your phone is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Data processed by Google through AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention periods. Your location is not kept. We do not store anything on a server ourselves.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Because we do not store data about you ourselves, most of this is handled directly on your phone (clear app data, reset advertising ID, Privacy settings) or with Google. Questions? Email us at the address above.

9. Children

Galactrivia is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Security

Your game data stays on your own device. Advertising data is sent encrypted by the Google SDK. Only in Pro mode of the sky map does the app, after an explanation, ask for your approximate location (see section 4). The app does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top. We mention major changes in the update notes on Google Play.
